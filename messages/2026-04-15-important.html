Our next mass will be on April 19 at 10.30 am at Cistercian. 

IMPORTANT:

Father Ignatius will become the Rector of the Cistercian house in Rome starting in the fall. Father Francis has stepped in to continue to offer masses in French to our community. We are very grateful to Father Abbott Peter for allowing this transition and for Father Francis’s enthusiasm to take on this additional responsibility. 

Please come to our next mass in April to thank Father Ignatius for his many years of dedication to our French-speaking masses and to wish him well as he embarks on this new chapter of his life in Rome. 

Father Francis and 3 other monks will travel to the Cistercian Abbey of Boulaur in June to improve their French and attend a special course on Gregorian Chant. Father Abbott Peter has asked for our financial support for their trip. We will have a special collection after the next 2 masses to help them out. Furthermore, please let us know if you have AA miles available for this purpose.
