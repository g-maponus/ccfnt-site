    •  Abraham & Jephta's sacrifices. May 2nd, Dallas Aldredge House, 6pm
Join us for an unforgettable evening with candlelights exploring the fascinating stories of Abraham and Jephta's sacrifices. Dive into these two sacred stories composed by Marc-Antoine Charpentier and Giacomo Carissimi. It's a perfect chance to connect, reflect, and share with others who are curious about these epic tales. The Lafayette Musicians, conducted by Matthieu Du Motier, will make you travel back to the XVIIIth century, singing this 300-year-old music. Don't miss out on this inspiring experience after having enjoyed a delicious French cocktail: (food, drinks- French wine included!) from 6 p.m. to 7 p.m.

  • Angela is singing with the Art District Chorale on May 8 at 8 p.m. on the theme: Voices in Blue. This will take place at the Lovers Lane United Methodist Church on Inwood Road.
  • Saturday, July 11, Potluck for Bastille Day at Tom and Angela’s home starting at 7 p.m.
