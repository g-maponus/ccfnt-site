A new initiative...Feedback appreciated!

8 février 2026
GOAL
To create a DESIGNATED FUND, through the Catholic Foundation, to allow Cistercian monks to perfect their French language to benefit the French-speaking audience attending monthly masses at Cistercian.
BACKGROUND
Over 25 years ago, Father Bernard MARTON accepted to lead a mass in French for the French-speaking community in Dallas and received the authorization to do so from Abbott Denis. Father Denis favored French, not only because of CITEAUX where the order was created, but also because he wrote his own thesis in French. The “French ” spirit has continued with the current Abbott Peter and is expected to be a durable legacy.
Nearly 10 years ago, Father Ignatius took over as Father Bernard was deployed to Hungary to assist the Cistercian order over there.
More recently, Father Francis returning from Rome and with a prior French experience with the Cistercian order at Hauterive (Fribourg) in Switzerland has joined the celebration of the monthly mass with Father Ignatius.
The talents of our current monks and their ability to speak French is impressive. However, during the year, they seldom speak French outside of the monthly masses.
Father Ignatius leads our Teams of Our Lady (Equipe Notre-Dame/END) which meets for dinner and an evening of exchange around texts provided by END to the participating couples. This is another occasion, on average every 2 months, to speak French.
Another resource in Dallas is the French Alliance or Alliance Francaise. But this would take additional commitments (driving and attending afternoon or evening sessions/time for remote course) and is impractical.
Finally, we have three potlucks every year (September, February and June/July) during which our monks are invited and can freely speak French all evening long. But this is not enough to really make progress.
In France, there have been prior exchanges with the Cistercian sisters in Boulaur and with other orders. The travel costs could be covered by this fund.  In the future as the fund grows by itself or through additional donations, possibly more monks could benefit of this resource. Conversely, the funds could be used to bring a Cistercian brother or sister to Dallas at any time during the year to allow daily interaction in French.
Of note, in addition to celebrating masses every month, both Father Ignatius and Father Francis offer the sacrament of reconciliation in French for those who prefer do have a confession in their native language.
  
PROPOSAL
DESIGNATED FUND managed by the Catholic Foundation
  1. Fund has a philanthropic purpose.
Purpose: Keeping interested Cistercian Monks fluent in French to celebrate mass to the French-speaking community in Dallas.
  1. Tax deduction for donors. The Catholic foundation is a 501(c)3 organization and donations are tax deductible
  2. There is an Advisory Committee composed of a minimum of 3 people (can be more).
              This committee needs to meet a few times yearly and has 2 tasks:
1) determine the type of investment for the funds
2) make sure that the distributed funds are in keeping with the stated philanthropy purpose of the Fund
  1. The annual fee for the Catholic Foundation to run the Fund is 1,1%
  2. If the funds are below 100K, we will use LCG, an independent advisor.
  3. If the funds exceed 100K, we can but are not obligated to use an outside management fund. We can stay with an independent advisor, in this case LCG which is the one used by Cistercian. They have 5 types of management funds to choose from.
  4. We can develop a Website with a dedicated Donate Now button.
  5.  The initial funding goal has been set at $100,000. With a 4% return on average, this will provide $4,000 yearly to offset travel costs and some basic living expenses. Miles for travel can be donated by anyone interested to support their travel expenses in addition to the above funds from this designated fund. 
NOTE:
As long as these funds are used for their intended purpose, these funds will be made available upon request to Father Abbott Peter at Cistercian or whomever succeeds him as Abbott of the Monastery; but they will not be directed by him.
We could annually inquire of the current Abbott if he anticipates a need for funds to provide support for the French Mass in its various ways. In turn we can let the current Abbott know that if he need funds at any point should a need arise we would most likely look favorably upon their request if it fits our goals.
SUMMARY:  The goal of this Designated Fund is to provide a sustainable funding mechanism to encourage Cistercian monks interested in perfecting their French knowledge and speech delivery to be fully immersed in a French-speaking environment.
